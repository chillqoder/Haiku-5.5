III · Four bars, no sprints

Method, in four bars.

  1. 1

    Listen

    We ask what the thing should feel like before we ask what it should do. Two hours with the people who will swear at it later.

    Adagio · a week, at most

  2. 2

    Sketch

    A rough, ugly prototype by Thursday. Nothing ships from a sketch, but everything gets argued with while it is still working.

    Allegro · three days

  3. 3

    Sing

    Put it in front of real hands. We watch where people hesitate and tune the parts that buzz. Changes are judged by feel first, numbers second.

    Moderato · until it hums

  4. 4

    Tune

    Refactor until nothing rattles. Then we write the test that would have caught the rattle, and leave the code better than we found it.

    Fermata · and then we stop
